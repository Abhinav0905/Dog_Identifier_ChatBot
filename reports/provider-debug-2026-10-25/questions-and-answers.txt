Question: It is very hot in Nagpur. What food and water can I safely leave for community dogs?
Answer: Leave fresh, clean water in wide, heavy bowls placed in shade, away from traffic. Use several bowls, refill often, and wash daily.
Feed during cooler morning or evening hours. Suitable options include dog food, or plain cooked rice with boiled egg or boneless chicken—without salt, spices, oil, onion or garlic.
Give small portions and remove leftovers promptly, as food spoils quickly in Nagpur’s heat. Avoid milk and cooked bones.
Place food calmly at a regular safe spot; give unfamiliar dogs space rather than calling or hand-feeding them.
If a dog is staggering, vomiting, collapsing or panting heavily, move water and shade nearby, cool its body gently with cool water, and seek urgent veterinary care. Do not force it to drink.
Judge: Recommends shade, fresh water and suitable food; warns about spoilage, milk and cooked bones, and avoids forced drinking.
